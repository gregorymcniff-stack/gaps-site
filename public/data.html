Columns in the readings

Times are UTC. A blank cell means the value was not available at that time; GAPS never fills gaps. Probabilities are between 0 and 1.

ColumnMeaning
time_utcWhen the reading was taken (every 10 minutes).
score_1to10The dashboard score, 1–10 (5 = average): weighted average of the three input scores below. Blank in readings from before the 1–10 update (14 Nov 2026).
score_tod_1to10, score_recent_programs_1to10, score_hub_weather_1to10Each input as a 1–10 score (its rank among past hours × 10, so 5 is average).
sfi_pctUnderlying chance behind the GAPS score, 0–100 (column name kept from its earlier name, Sky Friction Index): p_rough_3h × 100. Blank before the tested model went live (14 Nov 2026, about 04:20 UTC).
p_rough_3hEstimated chance that 3 or more new FAA ground stops or ground delay programs start at US airports in the next 3 hours.
p_any_program_3hEstimated chance of at least one new US ground stop or ground delay program in the next 3 hours.
typical_p_rough_3hThe p_rough_3h expected at that hour of the day (weekday or weekend) with average weather and activity.
hub_weather_pts_next3hIndex input: bad-weather points summed over 15 US hubs, worst hour of the next 3 (Open-Meteo forecast).
new_programs_last3hIndex input: new US ground stops and ground delay programs in the current and previous two hours (FAA advisory database).
faa_ground_stops, faa_ground_delay_programs, faa_airspace_flow_programs, faa_other_delaysPrograms in effect at the time, from FAA NAS Status.
faa_program_loadCount-based load score (0–100) of the programs in effect.
faa_affected_airportsAirports with a program or full closure in effect, separated by semicolons.
aircraft_tracked, aircraft_sourceAircraft count and its source: opensky (airborne, OpenSky) or adsblol (all tracked aircraft, adsb.lol). The two are not directly comparable.
metar_vfr, metar_mvfr, metar_ifr, metar_lifrNumber of monitored airports in each flight category in their latest METAR (23 airports at first; 26 from the per-airport update on 14 Nov 2026).
sigmet_thunderstorm, sigmet_turbulence, sigmet_icing, sigmet_volcanic_ash, sigmet_otherActive SIGMETs worldwide by hazard (international SIGMETs plus US convective and non-convective SIGMETs).
kp_indexNOAA planetary K-index (0–9).
score_1to10_JFK … score_1to10_LAXPer-airport 1–10 score shown on the dashboard, where 5 is average for that airport (15 columns). Blank in readings from before this update.
p_program_3h_JFK … p_program_3h_LAXPer-airport outlook: chance that the FAA starts a new ground stop or ground delay program at that airport in the next 3 hours (15 columns). Blank before 14 Nov 2026.

Columns in the FAA advisories file

ColumnMeaning
advisory_date, advisory_numberThe ATCSCC advisory's date and its number that day.
facility, airportThe facility as listed (for example ORD/ZAU) and the airport part.
title, sent_utcThe advisory title and when it was sent.
counted_as_new_us_program, program_type1 if GAPS counts it as a new US ground stop (GS) or ground delay program (GDP): cancellations, proposals, revisions, extensions and non-US facilities are excluded.
