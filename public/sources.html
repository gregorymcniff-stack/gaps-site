Terms of use

  • Information only. GAPS is a free public tool for situational awareness. It is not an official source, not a safety system, and must not be used for flight planning, dispatch or operational decisions.
  • No guarantee. Data can be late, incomplete or wrong, and feeds can fail. The outlook is a statistical estimate with known limits, described on the Method page.
  • No affiliation. GAPS has no affiliation with any airline, airport, the FAA, NOAA, EUROCONTROL or the data providers above.
  • Privacy. GAPS sets no cookies and uses no tracking or advertising. The hosting provider (Netlify) keeps standard server logs.

Reuse

If you reuse data shown here, follow each provider's licence above, including credit to Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0) and adsb.lol (ODbL). The backtest scripts and results are in the source repository.
