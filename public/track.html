How this is checked

  • Once an hour, the site takes the outlook it showed at the start of each past hour (the chance of a bad stretch: 3 or more new FAA ground stops or ground delay programs at US airports in the next 3 hours).
  • It then reads the FAA's own record of programs (the ATCSCC Advisory Database) and counts what really started in those 3 hours. This is the same test used on two years of past data, now run on live readings the model never saw.
  • An hour is counted only once its 3 hours are over and the FAA record for them could be read.
  • "How well it sorts hours" is the chance that a randomly chosen bad hour got a higher score than a randomly chosen calm one. 50% is a coin flip, 100% is perfect.
  • The comparison with "time of day only" shows whether the live readings (recent programs and hub weather) add anything beyond the usual daily pattern.

The model was refitted on 20 November 2026; hours before that used the earlier fit. Data: /api/track-record (JSON).
