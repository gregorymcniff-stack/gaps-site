      About GAPS

      GAPS watches the airline network and estimates how likely US airports are to hit a bad stretch of disruption in the next few hours. Every number on this page comes from a live public feed and shows its age. When a feed is down, its panel says so; nothing is filled in.

      The Sky Friction Index is built from the three things that predicted disruption in 120 days of FAA records: programs that have just started, forecast weather at major US hubs, and the time of day. An earlier version, built from world traffic, weather at 8 world airports and jet-stream winds, did no better than a coin flip in the same test and was replaced.

      GAPS is a free public tool with no affiliation to any airline, government agency or aviation authority. It is not a safety system and does not predict individual flights.
